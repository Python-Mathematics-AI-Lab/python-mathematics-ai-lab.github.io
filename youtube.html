LONG-TERM VISION

The goal is not simply to upload videos. The goal is to build a searchable, organized and sustainable academic learning library.

PHASE 01

Build the Foundation

Establish consistent branding, playlists, lecture naming and faculty contribution standards.

PHASE 02

Build the Library

Publish complete courses, lecture series, tutorials and practical projects.

PHASE 03

Reach Students

Use Shorts, concept videos, playlists and course links to increase discovery.

PHASE 04

Add seminars, research discussions, workshops, guest lectures and projects.
