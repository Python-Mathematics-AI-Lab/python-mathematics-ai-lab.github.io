ONE OFFICIAL CHANNEL

From Eight Faculty Classrooms to One Global Classroom

PMAI Lab operates one central academic YouTube channel. Faculty members contribute educational videos according to their subjects and areas of expertise.

A designated channel administrator manages video submission, quality review, uploading, titles, thumbnails, playlists, descriptions and publication.

This model keeps the channel organized, professional and consistent while giving every faculty member a recognized contribution within the PMAI Lab platform.

PMAI LAB PRINCIPLES

Build Together. Teach Together. Grow Together.

The PMAI Lab YouTube channel is a collaborative academic initiative. Faculty contributions remain connected to the contributor's academic expertise while being published under one institutional-style channel identity.

The objective is not simply to create videos. The objective is to build a structured digital learning library that students can use semester after semester.
