HOW THE CHANNEL WORKS

A Centralized Academic Publishing Model

Eight faculty members contribute educational content, while one designated channel administrator manages the official PMAI Lab YouTube channel.

01

Faculty Creates

A faculty contributor prepares a lecture, tutorial, practical session, research discussion or educational video.

02

Content Submission

The completed video is submitted to the designated PMAI Lab YouTube administrator for review and publishing.

03

Academic Review

The content is checked for academic quality, technical clarity, metadata and presentation standards.

04

Publishing

The channel administrator uploads the video with the approved title, description, thumbnail and tags.

05

Playlist Mapping

Each video is assigned to the appropriate subject, course and learning playlist.

06

Student Access

Students and public learners access the organized content through the official PMAI Lab channel.

FACULTY SUBMISSION WORKFLOW

From Faculty Video to Published Lecture

A simple centralized workflow will allow all eight faculty members to contribute without giving everyone direct control over the official YouTube channel.

01 — Record
Faculty member records and prepares the academic video.
02 — Submit
Video and required information are submitted to the channel administrator.
03 — Review
Content quality, title, description and academic information are checked.
04 — Publish
Administrator uploads the approved video to the official channel.
05 — Organize
Video is added to the correct course and subject playlist.
06 — Track
Contribution and performance data are recorded for PMAI Lab.
