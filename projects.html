OUR APPROACH

From Mathematical Ideas to Intelligent Systems

PMAI Lab develops projects that connect mathematical foundations with modern computational methods.

Our work includes Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, mathematical modeling, scientific computing, data science, educational technology and practical student projects.

Projects may involve theoretical investigation, software development, experimental research or real-world applications.

PMAI Project Principles

01

Mathematical Foundation

Use mathematics to understand, formulate and improve computational models.

02

Practical Implementation

Convert theoretical concepts into working Python, AI and data-driven applications.

03

Research Thinking

Encourage experimentation, evaluation, reproducibility and scientific reasoning.

04

Student Innovation

Give students opportunities to develop meaningful academic and technology projects.
