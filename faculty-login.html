HOW IT WORKS

Faculty Content Workflow

The PMAI Lab faculty portal is designed around a simple academic publishing workflow.

01

Faculty Login

Faculty members securely access their assigned course workspace.

02

Faculty Profile

Add faculty name, profile photo, department and academic information.

03

Upload Content

Upload lectures, assignments, quizzes, past papers and resources.

04

Publish Course

Your course remains hosted on your separate faculty GitHub account and is linked with PMAI Lab.

SECURITY & ACCESS

Faculty-only academic workspace

Only authorized faculty members should access the publishing system. Login credentials must never be stored directly inside frontend HTML or JavaScript.
